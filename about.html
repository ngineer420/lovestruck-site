About Lovestruck.us

Lovestruck.us is a small, independent site I write about the practical side of romance: what to do on a date night when you've run out of ideas, what to buy for an anniversary you're not sure how to mark, and how to handle the ordinary, unglamorous parts of a relationship that never make it into movies — like staying close across a long distance, or planning something as nerve-wracking as a proposal.

There's no editorial team or content mill behind this site — it's just me. It's a focused collection of guides, written to be genuinely useful on their own, whether or not you ever click a product link. I'd rather publish six guides worth reading twice than sixty guides worth reading once.

How the site is run

Lovestruck.us is a static website with no account system, no comments section, and no tracking beyond basic, privacy-respecting hosting logs (see my Privacy Policy for details). Some articles include affiliate links to products I think are genuinely relevant to the topic at hand — full details are in my Affiliate Disclosure. Affiliate income, if and when it exists, helps cover the (very small) cost of keeping the site running. It never determines what I choose to write about or what advice I give.

Corrections and feedback

If something on the site is inaccurate, outdated, or just plain unhelpful, I want to know. This is a young, small site, and it improves fastest with real feedback from real readers.

— Max, the person behind Lovestruck.us
