About Lovestruck.us

Lovestruck.us is a small, independent site about the practical side of romance: what to do on a date night when you've run out of ideas, what to buy for an anniversary you're not sure how to mark, and how to handle the ordinary, unglamorous parts of a relationship that never make it into movies — like staying close across a long distance, or planning something as nerve-wracking as a proposal.

We don't have a large editorial team or a content mill behind this site. It's a focused collection of guides, written to be genuinely useful on their own, whether or not you ever click a product link. We'd rather have six guides worth reading twice than sixty guides worth reading once.

How the site is run

Lovestruck.us is a static website with no account system, no comments section, and no tracking beyond basic, privacy-respecting hosting logs (see our Privacy Policy for details). Some articles include affiliate links to products we think are genuinely relevant to the topic at hand — full details are in our Affiliate Disclosure. Affiliate income, if and when it exists, helps cover the (very small) cost of keeping the site running. It never determines what we choose to write about or what advice we give.

Corrections and feedback

If something on the site is inaccurate, outdated, or just plain unhelpful, we want to know. This is a young, small site, and it improves fastest with real feedback from real readers.
